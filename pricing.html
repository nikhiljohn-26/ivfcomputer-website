FAQ
How is the 2% calculated?
2% of your clinic's collected revenue, billed monthly on actuals. BillingOS tracks every invoice, so the number is transparent and auditable by your own team.
What if we have a slow month?
Then our invoice is smaller too. There's no minimum, no fixed floor. If your clinic earns less, we earn less. This keeps incentives clean.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
No. We run 60-day pilots. After that, it's month-to-month. No exit penalties.
Can I start with just one module?
All 13 modules are included from day one — you don't pay per module. Start with the ones most relevant to your workflow.
