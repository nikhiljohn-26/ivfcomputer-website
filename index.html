Why clinics use it

A fertility clinic runs on WhatsApp and memory.

Nobody is careless. There's simply too much for any team to hold in their heads.

The inbox
"Did anyone reply to her?"

Enquiries, worried questions and reports all land in one WhatsApp. Messages get buried, and patients wait.

The reports
"Whose report is this?"

Labs send PDFs from their own numbers, named any way. Someone has to open each one and work out who it belongs to.

The history
"What did we try last time?"

Across hundreds of couples, no doctor can remember every earlier cycle, every result, every pattern.

What it does

Everything your clinic does, in one place.

One system for your doctors, coordinators, embryologists and front desk, each seeing only what they need.

💬

Messages

Every WhatsApp in one shared inbox, longest wait first. AI drafts the reply, and your team checks it before it goes.

📄

Reports

Lab reports are read and filed to the right patient. When it isn't sure whose report it is, it asks instead of guessing.

EMR

Cycles and lab
