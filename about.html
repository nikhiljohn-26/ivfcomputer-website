Our story

We went through it. Then we fixed it.

IVFComputer was built by a couple who lived the IVF journey as patients, and came out of it determined to make it less chaotic for everyone who comes after.

The beginning

The checklist no one followed.

Nikhil and Nakita went through fertility treatment at an IVF clinic in Mumbai. Nakita, an archivist by training, documented everything: every protocol, every checklist, every form.

Their first IUI failed. Not because the medicine was wrong, and not because the doctor made a mistake. A follow-up call didn't happen. A day-3 reminder slipped. A coordinator had 40 patients and no system, just memory and good intentions.

What Nakita found was chaos disguised as process: paper forms, WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, and five separate tools for one patient's journey. Every missed step was a person doing their best without the right support.

After their IVF cycle succeeded, they built the system the clinic should have had. Every part of IVFComputer traces back to something they lived through.
